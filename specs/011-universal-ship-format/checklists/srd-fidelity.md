# SRD-Fidelity Checklist: Universal Ship Description Format

**Purpose**: Requirements-quality review gate for Constitution Principle I — validate that every wording, tech level and derived figure the requirements call for is traceable to a stated SRD source, that each deviation is recorded with a rationale, and that no data is invented where the SRD states none. These items test the *requirements and their sourcing*, not the implementation.
**Created**: 2026-07-24
**Feature**: [spec.md](../spec.md)

**Scope of review**: `spec.md` with `research.md`, `data-model.md` and `contracts/description-format.md` as requirement sources. The constitution requires SRD ambiguity to be resolved and documented in research.md, and any deliberate deviation to be recorded in the plan's Constitution Check.

**Cross-check status (2026-07-24)**: All 33 items reviewed. **All 33 resolved** — 13 required a `spec.md` edit, made the same day; the other 20 were already sourced in `research.md`. Two findings were structural rather than clerical: FR-030 forbade nothing about inventing a *display name*, though FR-028a forbade inventing a tech level (CHK012); and the spec's worked-examples-win assumption covered phrasing only, leaving no rule for an example that contradicts a stated rule (CHK003, CHK026). After the amendments, **one** knowing departure from the SRD's examples remains — the 11–99 number band (CHK015) — plus the small-craft cockpit correction (CHK017), which departs from a self-contradictory passage rather than from a rule.
